Shamim

AI Trainer · Founder & CEO, SparkUp Creative LLC

Yaser founded SparkUp Creative to prove one idea: AI is not for the elite. It belongs to everyone willing to learn.

With a BCA and an MBA in HR, he has always worked where technology meets people. When AI arrived he saw the biggest opportunity of his generation, and built a company around teaching everyday people, especially those without big degrees or connections, to earn real income with AI-powered content systems.

Today he leads SparkUp's AI training programs and the SparkUp Creator Academy, builds AI systems for businesses, oversees every brand in the SparkUp family, and runs the Women Empowerment Initiative, which gives free certified AI training to women from underserved communities.
