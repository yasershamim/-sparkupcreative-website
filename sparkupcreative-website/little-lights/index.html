About the Author

Yasham H.

Yasham H. is the creator of Little Lights, a series of gentle, Bible-based coloring and activity books for children ages 3 to 6. Each book helps the youngest children discover God's love in a way they can understand: big, simple pictures for small hands, Bible verses retold in easy words, and short stories that show how faith looks in everyday life.

Yasham's hope is simple: that every child who opens a Little Lights book will close it knowing they are deeply and completely loved by God.

What ages are the books for?

Ages 3 to 6. The pictures have thick lines and large shapes, and the Bible verses are written in simple words. Older siblings enjoy the activities too.

Can I buy from outside the USA?

Yes. Every link on this page opens Amazon in your own country, including the UK, Canada, Australia and much of Europe.

When should I order for Christmas?

The books are printed when you order, so allow a few extra days. For delivery before Christmas, order by mid-December and check Amazon's delivery date at checkout.

Can I print the free pages for my class?

Yes. The free Christmas pages are free to print for home, church and classroom use.

Which Bible verses are used?

Each verse is short and simple, and marked with its Bible reference so you can read the full passage together.
